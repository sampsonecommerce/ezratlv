Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting Ezra's public events.

Each event has a clear status: events tagged as Free Entry are walk-in friendly on a first-come, first-served basis. For special events like guest chef pop-ups or limited-capacity album listening sessions, advance RSVP is recommended or required via the button on the card.
The weekly schedule on this page updates in real time. Dates booked for private gatherings are clearly marked in red ("Private Event"), and open nights display operating hours and featured programs.
A full craft cocktail bar, natural and boutique wines, cold beers, and hot stone-oven dishes (focaccias, savory pastries, charred vegetables, fresh fish crudo and seasonal specials).
Ezra is located at 38 Abarbanel Street, in the heart of Florentin, Tel Aviv. Paid parking lots are nearby on Eliphelet and Herzl streets, and the Red Line light rail (Salame/Eliphelet station) is a short walk away.
