Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about visiting Ezra for public events.

Each event card has a clear badge: Free Entry nights are open for walk-ins on a first-come, first-served basis. Reservation Required events (like chef pop-ups or limited capacity sessions) require a quick table reservation in advance via the button on this page.
The This Week at Ezra schedule at the top of this page is updated live from our booking system. Dates booked for private gatherings are marked in red as "Closed for Private Event", while open nights display operating hours and planned programs.
A full craft cocktail bar, natural and boutique wines, ice cold beers, and freshly baked bites from our wood-fired stone oven (focaccias, savory pastries, roasted market veggies, crudos and chef small plates).
Ezra is located at 38 Abarbanel Street, in the heart of Florentin, Tel Aviv. Parking garages are available on Elifelet and Herzl streets, and the Red Line Light Rail (Shalma/Elifelet station) is just a short walk away.
